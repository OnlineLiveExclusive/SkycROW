UI/UX Principles for Casual Web Games

Design moves that turn new visitors into repeat players.

Published 2025-08-22

Design moves that turn new visitors into repeat players.

Clear affordances

Buttons look like buttons; controls are labeled on first run.

Short loops

Restart in one tap; show best score to motivate another try.

Gentle difficulty ramp

Add complexity after the first success, not before.
